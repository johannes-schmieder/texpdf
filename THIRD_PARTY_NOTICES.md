# Third-party notices

The canonical redistribution and notice material is maintained in:

- [`licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md);
- [`docs/LICENSING.md`](docs/LICENSING.md);
- the generated dependency and embedded-resource inventories produced by the release tooling.

Project-owned source is MIT licensed. Tectonic, Rust dependencies, native libraries, TeX/LaTeX resources, fonts, encodings, maps, and bibliography styles retain their upstream licenses.

Public binary publication remains fail-closed until every embedded resource is mapped to reviewed license evidence and every required notice is included in the release package.
